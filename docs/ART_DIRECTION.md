# DOMINION OF SOL — CANONICAL ART DIRECTION & CULTURAL HERITAGE ATLAS
## Global Civilization Visual Vocabulary & Material Culture Specification

This document defines the authoritative art direction, material palettes, architecture, military silhouettes, and cultural design guidelines for all 44 historical civilizations in the Dominion of Sol Global Atlas.

---

## 1. General Principles & Cultural Respect Guidelines

1. **Grounded Historical Authenticity**: Visuals are derived from verified archaeological, architectural, textile, and material culture sources. Avoid 19th-century colonial romanticism, Hollywood caricature, and generic "fantasy warrior" tropes.
2. **No Faux Modern Flags**: Pre-modern civilizations did not fly modern national tricolors or rectangular state flags. For civilizations without an attested state banner, Dominion of Sol employs **fictional gameplay heraldic standards and pennants** inspired by authentic material culture, treated explicitly as game identity art rather than historical claims.
3. **Sacred Iconography Protection**:
   - Do not trivialize living sacred motifs, initiation symbols, burial totems, or spiritual body markings as decorative stickers or generic button icons.
   - For Indigenous cultures (Lakota, Haudenosaunee, Diné, Inuit, Haida, Yolŋu, Arrernte, Noongar, Māori): Focus on **landscape, architecture, ecological adaptation, craftsmanship, woven textiles, and community structures** rather than warpaint, loose feathers, or weapons.
4. **Distinct Steppe Identities**:
   - HUN and GÖKTÜRK must remain visually, materially, and chronologically distinct.
   - HUN: Late Antiquity western steppe equestrian aristocracy; composite reflex bows, scale/lamellar harness, felt/leather/raw bronze, mobile felt tents.
   - GÖKTÜRK: 6th–8th century Central Asian steppe khaganate; celestial azure and iron palette, runic/tamga standard heraldry, structured elite cavalry coats, silver-inlaid belt plaques.
   - OTTOMAN IMPERIAL: 16th–18th century metropolis of the Sublime Porte; crimson silk, Iznik ceramics, kilij blades, turban court attire, Janissary heraldry. This remains exclusive to the Ottoman Imperial cosmetic family in Sovereign Armory and is never mislabeled as Hun or Göktürk.

## 2. Global Civilizations Directory (44 Identities)

### EUROPE (5)

#### 1. ROMA (`roma`)
- **Core**: Italian Peninsula / Central Mediterranean.
- **Materials**: Travertine, white Carrara marble, fired Roman brick, Roman concrete (opus caementicium), bronze, hammered iron.
- **Architecture**: Segmental arches, barrel vaults, basilicas, classical porticoes, aqueducts, castra fortress layouts.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Segmented lorica segmentata / lorica hamata, scutum rectangular curved shields, cassis helmets with transverse crests, pilum javelins.
- **Clothing**: Undyed wool tunics, crimson and Tyrian purple bordered togas, paludamentum cloaks.
- **Color Family**: Imperial crimson (`#7a1828`), warm Roman gold (`#cda851`), aged marble white, bronze patina.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Roman legionary Aquila (eagle standard), laurel wreath coronas, ceremonial fasces.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Hollywood horned helmets, Renaissance fantasy armor, generic medieval castle battlements.

#### 2. HELLEN (`hellen`)
- **Core**: Aegean Basin / Mainland Greece.
- **Materials**: Pentelic marble, poros limestone, olive wood, high-tin bronze, painted terracotta.
- **Architecture**: Doric and Ionic peripteral temples, amphitheaters, stoas, acropolis citadels.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Corinthian/Chalcidian helmets, bronze bell cuirasses, round aspis/hoplon shields with geometric blazons, dory thrusting spears.
- **Clothing**: Draped linen and wool chitons, himation mantles, leather sandals.
- **Color Family**: Aegean maritime cyan (`#1e4e6f`), terracotta orange, sun-bleached ivory, bright bronze.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Laurel crowns, panathenaic amphorae, tripod cauldrons.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Spartan leather undergarment tropes, Roman legionary gear, blackened gothic plate.

#### 3. GAUL (`gaul`)
- **Core**: Gaul / Western Continental Celtic sphere.
- **Materials**: Oak timber, river clay, iron chainmail, enameled bronze, spun wool.
- **Architecture**: Murus Gallicus (timber-laced stone ramparts), fortified oppida hilltop towns, thatched roundhouses.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Tall oval wooden shields with bronze spine bosses, iron longswords (La Tène style), carnyx war horns, horned bronze helmets (ceremonial).
- **Clothing**: Richly dyed checkered/plaid wool tunics, braccae (trousers), heavy wool cloaks fastened by bronze fibulae.
- **Color Family**: Forest moss green (`#1a4329`), woad indigo (`#1c385c`), bronze gold, autumn rust.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Twisted gold and bronze torcs, bronze carnyx horns with boar heads.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Cartoon Asterix winged helmets, naked savage tropes, Druidic fantasy staves.

#### 4. NORSE (`norse`)
- **Core**: Scandinavian Peninsula / North Atlantic fringe.
- **Materials**: Pine and birch timber, forged bog iron, walrus ivory, wool wadmal, seal and reindeerskin.
- **Architecture**: Wooden turf-roofed longhouses, stave structures, boathouses (nausts), stave palisades.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Conical iron spangenhelms with spectacle/nasal guards, riveted mail byrnies, round painted pine shields, bearded axes, pattern-welded broadswords.
- **Clothing**: Linen undertunics, woolen overtunics, kyrtill robes, oval brooch-pinned apron dresses, sheepskin mantles.
- **Color Family**: Deep fjord slate blue (`#1d4872`), pine bark brown, silver steel, bone white.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Carved ship prow dragons (drekkar), silver arm rings, carved rune stones.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Horned helmets (strictly non-historical), fur bikini barbarians, biker leather jackets.

#### 5. RUS (`rus`)
- **Core**: East European forest-steppe / Dnieper-Volga riverways.
- **Materials**: White birch, carved oak, forged river iron, fur, flax linen, river pearls.
- **Architecture**: Wooden kremlins with log towers, rubleny izba timber compounds, tent-roofed early churches.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Tall pointed conical helmets with chain aventails, lamellar and scale armor coats, teardrop kite shields, sabers and composite bows.
- **Clothing**: Fur-trimmed kaftans, loose linen shashovers, felt boots, silver-threaded sashes.
- **Color Family**: River amber gold (`#b8860b`), birch bark cream, deep Dnieper blue (`#152d4a`), crimson trim.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Silver barmas (beaded collars), grivna neck rings, carved wooden river idols.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Modern Soviet or Cossack 19th-century uniforms, stereotypical snow shovels, fantasy ice magic.

#### 10. YORUBA (`yoruba`)
- **Core**: Gulf of Guinea / Southwestern West Africa.
- **Materials**: Cast cire-perdue (lost wax) brass/bronze, terracota, glass seed beads, mahogany wood.
- **Architecture**: Rectangular courtyard compounds (Agbo Ile) with impluvium drainage, carved verandah posts (Opo), polished red clay floors.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Quilted cotton armor, iron-bladed short spears, curved iron billhooks, horsemen with leather-covered shields.
- **Clothing**: Richly patterned Aso Oke woven textiles, embroidered Agbada robes, beaded coronets.
- **Color Family**: Forest coral orange (`#c2410c`), indigo blue (`#1e3a8a`), patinated brass (`#b45309`), deep palm green.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Ife cast bronze portrait heads, beaded royal crowns (Ade) with hanging veils, carved ivory fly whisks.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Cartoon tribal mask tropes, generic savage jungle kitsch, plastic modern beads.

#### 11. KONGO (`kongo`)
- **Core**: Lower Congo River Basin / Western Central Africa.
- **Materials**: Woven raffia palm cloth (nzimpu), carved ivory, river iron, red copper, clay.
- **Architecture**: Geometric enclosure palisades, high-peaked woven thatch pavilions, broad public assembly plazas (mbazi).

- **Clothing**: Finely woven raffia velvet cloth with stepped geometric interlocking patterns, capes with leopard pelt collars.
- **Color Family**: Raffia palm ochre (`#b45309`), copper bronze (`#92400e`), river slate, deep umber.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Woven prestige caps (mpu), carved wooden scepters, spiral-incised copper torque collars.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Tarzan jungle stereotypes, generic leopard-print caveman costumes, colonial weaponry.

#### 12. SWAHILI (`swahili`)
- **Core**: East African Indian Ocean Coast.
- **Materials**: Porous coral stone (rag), mangrove timber, lime plaster, imported Chinese porcelain, sea silk.
- **Architecture**: Multi-story coral stone townhouses with decorative carved wooden doors, shaded courtyards, seaside crenelations.

#### 13. ZULU (`zulu`)
- **Core**: Southeastern Africa / Drakensberg foothills.

- **Architecture**: Circular royal kraals (isigodlo) with woven beehive grass lodges, defensive palisades.

- **Clothing**: Leather aprons (isinene/ibheshu), cow-tail arm and leg adornments, crane feather headbands.

- **Ceremonial Objects**: Regimental color-coded shields, royal carved wooden headrests, polished wooden meat platters.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Cartoon cannibal stewpots, barefoot helpless savage tropes, fantasy voodoo skulls.

#### 30. ĐẠI VIỆT (`dai_viet`)

- **Ceremonial Objects**: Dong Son decorated bronze drums, stone turtle stelae holding doctor laureates, Bodhi leaf terracotta ridge crests.

#### 31. MAJAPAHIT (`majapahit`)

### NORTH AMERICA (5)

#### 32. LAKOTA (`lakota`)
- **Core**: Northern Great Plains / Black Hills (Paha Sapa).
- **Materials**: Tanned buffalo hide, lodgepole pine, porcupine quills, glass seed beads, river pipestone (catlinite).
- **Architecture**: Conical buffalo hide tipis with wooden smoke flaps, winter camp river bottoms, sun dance encampment rings.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Superb equestrian scouts riding bareback or with hide pads, short sinew-backed bows, wooden ball-headed war clubs, painted rawhide shields.
- **Clothing**: Tanned buckskin dresses and shirts adorned with porcupine quill embroidery and geometric seed beadwork, fringed leggings.
- **Color Family**: Red pipestone (`#7c2d12`), prairie sage green (`#4d7c0f`), morning sun yellow (`#facc15`), deep midnight slate.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Catlinite sacred pipes (chanunpa), winter count pictographic hide records, painted rawhide parflèche cases.
- **Cultural Respect**: Strictly avoid loose war bonnets on every figure, random face-paint lines, caricature tomahawk stances. Ground in landscape and astronomy.

#### 33. HAUDENOSAUNEE (`haudenosaunee`)
- **Core**: Great Lakes / Northeastern Woodlands.
- **Materials**: Elm bark, hickory timber, quahog and whelk wampum shells, deer sinew, woven basswood fiber.
- **Architecture**: Communal barrel-roofed elm bark longhouses (Ganondagan), palisaded woodland hilltop towns.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Forest skirmishers with carved hardwood ball clubs with inset stone or iron spikes, hide-covered wooden shields, light elm longbows.
- **Clothing**: Deerskin tunics and skirts embroidered with dyed moose hair and beads, woven gustoweh headdresses with upright eagle and clan feathers.
- **Color Family**: Purple wampum shell (`#581c87`), forest elm bark brown (`#5c2c16`), deer hide tan, woodland fern green.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Wampum record belts (Hiawatha Belt depicting five nations linked by unity pine), carved condolence canes.
- **Cultural Respect**: Do NOT portray False Face Society sacred masks (these are private medicine society items never for public display). Focus on longhouse governance, council fires, and wampum belt diplomacy.

#### 34. DINÉ (`dine`)
- **Core**: American Southwest / Four Corners Colorado Plateau.
- **Materials**: Red sandstone, piñon and juniper wood, hand-spun Churro sheep wool, beaten silver, turquoise stone.
- **Architecture**: Multi-sided timber and earthen hogans oriented toward the east, drystone sheep corrals, mesa stone lookouts.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Mountain and canyon defense scouts, sinew-reinforced recurve bows, yucca fiber shields, woven rawhide quivers.
- **Clothing**: Churro wool woven chief blankets with bold striped and stepped diamond patterns, velveteen blouses, silver concho belts.
- **Color Family**: Canyon sandstone red (`#991b1b`), mountain turquoise (`#0284c7`), Churro natural wool black/cream, piñon green.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Hand-woven geometric textiles, silver and turquoise squash blossom necklaces, painted wedding baskets.
- **Cultural Respect**: Never depict permanent sacred sandpaintings (they are temporary curative ceremonies that must be dismantled). Focus on textile mastery, silversmithing, and the four sacred mountains.

#### 35. INUIT (`inuit`)
- **Core**: Arctic North America / Tundra and Coastal Ice.
- **Materials**: Caribou antler, marine ivory (walrus/narwhal), whalebone, seal and caribou skin, soapstone (steatite), drift wood.
- **Architecture**: Winter snow-brick iglu domes, summer sealskin tupiq tents, stone food caches, semi-subterranean turf and whalebone winter houses.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Sea mammal hunters with toggle-head ivory harpoons, recurve bows backed with braided sinew cable, bone slat armor (rare historical arctic armor).
- **Clothing**: Layered fur parkas (atigi and qalugaq), caribou skin mittens, waterproof bearded seal skin boots (kamiks), slit bone snow goggles.
- **Color Family**: Arctic glacier ice blue (`#38bdf8`), snow ivory (`#f8fafc`), caribou hide warm grey (`#64748b`), dark sea slate.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Inuksuk navigation stone landmarks, soapstone spirit carvings, tambourine-style frame drums (qilaut).
- **Cultural Respect**: Avoid "Eskimo" colonial tropes, fake cartoon igloos with chimneys, generic seal-clubbing depictions. Honor navigational mastery and survival technology.

#### 36. HAIDA (`haida`)
- **Core**: Pacific Northwest Coast / Haida Gwaii.
- **Materials**: Old-growth Western Red Cedar, argillite stone, mountain goat wool, shredded cedar bark, abalone pearl.
- **Architecture**: Massive post-and-beam red cedar plank longhouses with painted housefronts and monumental crest poles.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Ocean-going war canoe marines (50-foot dugout canoes), heavy slat and elk-hide armor, carved hardwood war daggers with abalone inlay.
- **Clothing**: Woven spruce root basketry hats with potlatch rings, Chilkat woven wool blankets with formline animal designs, cedar bark capes.
- **Color Family**: Traditional formline black (`#0f172a`), vermilion red (`#dc2626`), cedar bark ochre, abalone iridescence.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Monumental crest totem poles, carved raven rattles, argillite sculptural model houses.
- **Cultural Respect**: Do not invent random cartoon formline curves. Haida formline art follows strict, mathematically precise ovoid, u-form, and s-form rules. Respect clan crest lineage.

### SOUTH AMERICA (EXACTLY 4)

#### 37. INCA (`inca`)
- **Core**: Central Andes / Sacred Valley of Cusco.
- **Materials**: Finely cut ashlar stone (polygonal masonry with pillowed faces), alpaca and vicuña wool, cast bronze, beaten gold and silver.
- **Architecture**: Monumental drystone terrace agriculture, trapezoidal doors and niches, sun temples (Coricancha), high-altitude fortresses (Saksaywaman).
- **Military Silhouettes**: Imperial regiments with bronze star-headed maces (champi), woven slings (warak'a), palm-wood halberds, rectangular wooden shields painted with heraldic tokapu checkers.
- **Clothing**: Tightly woven unku tunics adorned with geometric tokapu badges indicating administrative rank, woven llautu headbands with red fringe (mascapaycha for the Sapa Inca).
- **Color Family**: Royal imperial gold (`#ca8a04`), Andean cochineal red (`#991b1b`), mountain stone grey (`#475569`), vicuña wool tan.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Khipu knot record systems, golden sun discs (Punchao), carved wooden kero drinking vessels.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Aztec feather headdress confusion, panpipe cartoon busker stereotypes, generic Mayan pyramid architecture.

#### 38. MUISCA (`muisca`)
- **Core**: Northern Andes / Altiplano Cundiboyacense (Colombia).
- **Materials**: Tumbaga (gold-copper alloy), emeralds, compressed clay, woven cotton, bamboo cane.
- **Architecture**: Circular chieftains' temples (bohios) with conical thatched roofs, wooden palisades, sacred lake offering platforms (Lake Guatavita).
- **Military Silhouettes**: Guecha warrior aristocracy armed with throwing darts, spearthrowers (atlatls), hardwood swords (macanas), wearing golden pectorals and nose rings.
- **Clothing**: White and red patterned cotton tunics (chircate) fastened at the shoulder, gold crown diadems.
- **Color Family**: Radiant tumbaga gold (`#eab308`), Muzo emerald green (`#059669`), mountain clay terracotta, cotton white.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Muisca golden raft (Balsa Muisca depicting El Dorado ceremony), votive tunjo figures, emerald offerings.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Conflation with Inca stone masonry (Muisca built primarily in timber and thatch), Spanish colonial conquistador armor.

#### 39. MAPUCHE (`mapuche`)
- **Core**: Southern Andes / Southern Cone / Araucanía.
- **Materials**: Native southern beech (roble/coigüe), hammered silver, hand-spun sheep wool, volcanic basalt, leather.
- **Architecture**: Large family ruka lodges made of timber poles thatched with reeds (quila), fortified frontier redoubts.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Unyielding cavalry and infantry resisting imperial expansion, long colihue wood pikes, hardened leather armor, bolas, rawhide lances.
- **Clothing**: Thick hand-woven indigo wool ponchos (makuñ), silver trapelacucha chest pendants, trarilonko silver coin headbands.
- **Color Family**: Patagonian deep indigo (`#1e3a8a`), Araucanía silver (`#94a3b8`), volcanic earth black, forest green.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Cultrún ritual wooden drums painted with four quarters of the cosmos, chemamüll carved ancestral wooden statues.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Hollywood Amazon jungle feathers, Inca road system imitation, generic Western movie tropes.

#### 40. GUARANÍ (`guarani`)
- **Core**: Paraná / Paraguay River Basins / Subtropical Interior.
- **Materials**: Subtropical timber, woven lianas, river clay, calabash gourds, river feathers.
- **Architecture**: Communal maloca longhouses accommodating extended kinship networks, shaded plaza villages surrounded by timber defensive ditches.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Expert forest archers using tall hard-palm bows, heavy hardwood clubs (yvyrapuku), poison dart blowguns, river canoes.
- **Clothing**: Woven vegetal fiber aprons, feather coronets for leaders, natural red urucum and black genipapo body patterns reflecting kinship lineages.
- **Color Family**: Forest canopy emerald (`#15803d`), urucum seed orange-red (`#c2410c`), river clay brown, palm gold.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Carved wooden animal guardians, ceremonial takuara rhythmic bamboo stampers, engraved mate gourds.
- **Elements to Avoid**: Stone temple architecture (strictly forest timber/thatch), cartoon cannibal stereotypes, modern tourist carnival costumes.

### AUSTRALIA (EXACTLY 3)

#### 41. YOLŊU (`yolngu`)
- **Core**: Northern Australia / Arnhem Land cultural sphere.
- **Materials**: Stringybark sheets, natural ground earth pigments (white pipeclay, red ochre, yellow ochre, charcoal), ironwood, pandanus fiber.
- **Architecture**: Elevated timber stilt platforms for wet season breezes, bark-roofed family shelters, coastal fish traps built of tidal stone.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Skilled coastal voyagers and spear-throwers using precision woomera spearthrowers, barbed fishing and hunting spears, light bark shields.
- **Clothing**: Natural earth ochre ceremonial body designs denoting clan and moiety (Dhuwa and Yirritja), woven pandanus mats and waistbands.
- **Color Family**: Deep red ochre (`#991b1b`), white pipeclay (`#f8fafc`), yellow earth ochre (`#ca8a04`), charcoal grey.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Yiḏaki (hollowed eucalyptus musical instrument / didgeridoo), carved hollow log memorial poles (Larrakitj), painted stringybark paintings.
- **Cultural Respect**: Never claim broad gameplay envelopes equal ethnographic boundaries. Avoid generic decorative dots; Yolŋu art is defined by **rarrk cross-hatching** belonging to specific clan lineages.

#### 42. ARRERNTE (`arrernte`)
- **Core**: Central Australia / MacDonnell Ranges / Red Desert.
- **Materials**: Hard red mulga wood, desert spinifex resin, river gum bark, ground stone pigments, emu feathers.
- **Architecture**: Seasonal spinifex and mulga bough shelters (wiltja), shaded red sandstone gorge campsites, protected rock art galleries.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Desert hunters with mulga wood spear throwers (amwerne), heavy fluted hardwood shields (atnwerte), fighting boomerangs.
- **Clothing**: Spun hair string belts, ceremonial ground-ochre body markings connecting individuals to sacred ancestral songlines (tjukurpa).
- **Color Family**: Red desert sand (`#b91c1c`), sunburnt ochre (`#d97706`), ghost gum white, desert night indigo.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Churinga sacred stone and wooden boards, fluted mulga carrying dishes (coolamon), ground earth ground-altars.
- **Cultural Respect**: Do NOT depict sacred churinga symbols or secret initiation motifs. Focus on landscape adaptation, water conservation, family shelters, and tools.

#### 43. NOONGAR (`noongar`)
- **Core**: Southwestern Australia / Swan Coastal Plain & Jarrah Forests.
- **Materials**: Jarrah and marri timber, balga (grasstree) resin, kangaroo skin (boodjar), paperbark, river reeds.
- **Architecture**: Dome-shaped mia-mia shelters framed with curved branches and lined with waterproof paperbark sheets and grasstree thatch.
- **Military Silhouettes**: Hunters with precision gidji throwing spears, wunda grooved shields painted with zig-zag ochre bands, wooden hunting boomerangs (kylie).
- **Clothing**: Buka booka warm kangaroo skin cloaks fastened at the shoulder, worn fur-side inward for winter warmth.
- **Color Family**: Coastal ocean turquoise (`#0d9488`), jarrah timber brown (`#572c14`), coastal sand ivory, wild red ochre.
- **Ceremonial Objects**: Wunda carved wooden shields with parallel incised zig-zag lines, stone tidal weir structures, carved message sticks.
- **Cultural Respect**: Avoid pan-Aboriginal homogenization. Represent the distinctive 6-season ecological calendar and coastal-forest adaptation.
