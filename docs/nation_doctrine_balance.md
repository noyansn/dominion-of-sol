# Dominion of Sol — Nation Doctrine and Balance Contract

Every nation carries four bounded zero-sum doctrine offsets in the
authoritative faction descriptor:

- Offense
- Defense
- Expansion
- Maritime

Offsets are clamped and normalized on player join so their sum is zero and no
axis exceeds six percentage points. These descriptors are a balancing and
identity surface; they do not bypass the Population ledger or authorize
illegal orders.

## Balance rules

- Population is the common cost and opportunity cost for humans and bots.
- Land capacity and growth come from real latitude-corrected controlled area.
- Ports have a permanent Population cost, a construction delay, and a modest
  completed-port growth bonus.
- Attack commitment is a percentage of the attacker's current free pool and
  has a server-enforced minimum deployment.
- Defense Focus uses the same real pool and cannot duplicate people already
  allocated to a local operation.
- Casualties are permanent; surviving deployed people return only when their
  operation ends.

No doctrine, nation descriptor, or future cosmetic purchase may mint
Population, ignore capacity, alter ownership semantics, or alter the server's
legal-point checks.
